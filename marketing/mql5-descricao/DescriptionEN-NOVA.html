Main input groups

  • TradeFrequency and RunStrat1–RunStrat9: activity and configuration selection; AllowBuyTrades and AllowSellTrades enable the entry directions.
  • Risk, StartLots, MaxAllowedDD and MaxRiskPerStrategy_: sizing method and exposure references. Volume can vary with capital, price scaling and sizing parameters. These are not guaranteed loss limits; rounding and minimum volume can exceed a reference.
  • UseVariableValues and AdjustLotsizeToVariableValues: price scaling and volume adjustment. For physical volume equal to StartLots in fixed mode, disable AdjustLotsizeToVariableValues. Auto uses thresholds in the nominal account currency; other currencies and cent accounts require their own settings.
  • MaxSpread, CheckMargin, ST1_MagicNumber and ST1_Comment: filtering and trade identification. MaxSpread uses a nominal price unit of 0.01, adjusted by the scale; it does not directly mean points in every quotation format. Mandatory margin reservation remains active with CheckMargin disabled.
  • FridayStopHour, FridayClosePending, FridayCloseOpen and PropFirmMaxDailyDD: Friday handling and configurable daily protection. Gaps and execution conditions can exceed risk references.
  • EnableNFP_Filter, EnableCPI_Filter and EnableIR_Filter: windows and actions for US news. Avoid overlapping magic numbers on the same symbol.

Compatibility and limits

The native calendar uses trade-server time. Missing or insufficient data pauses new orders; existing pending orders can still execute and position management continues. In the tester, or without the native calendar, embedded history covers 2010–2027 and requires manual broker time-zone settings. Windows outside coverage block entries; future dates can change, and the 2027 Fed calendar is tentative.

AutoGMT retains only a two-second startup delay; it does not calculate time zones. BacktestSpeed uses normal cadence in all modes. The reserve counts account-wide pending orders but cancels only its own; it can restrict activity and conservatively double-count margin already reserved by a broker. Missing data does not cause blind cancellation.

Sufficient margin does not limit losses. Leverage, gaps, slippage, costs and external changes can alter execution and results, including loss of capital.
